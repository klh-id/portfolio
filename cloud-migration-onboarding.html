Customer onboarding · Rapid recovery

Cloud-migration onboarding program

I recovered an undefined customer engagement, aligned a cross-functional team, and delivered 11 resources across two role-based learning paths in six weeks.

Timeline
Six weeks
Team
Two instructional designers plus cross-functional partners
Scope
11 resources across two role-based paths
Customer CSAT
100%

The challenge

When I joined, the customer engagement was already several weeks underway, but no one had defined the curriculum or even produced an outline. The delivery team needed clarity quickly so training could support the customer’s implementation timeline.

Program recovery plan

I assessed the implementation-related gaps and created a curriculum map for two role-based learning paths. That map established the scope, sequence, objectives, ownership, and review requirements needed to turn an ambiguous request into an executable program.

Leadership and execution

  • Scoped the content and objectives, assigned work to two instructional designers, and reviewed their deliverables.
  • Created program content while leading the broader delivery effort.
  • Aligned solutions architects, the customer education delivery team, and the customer.
  • Provided weekly progress updates internally and directly to the customer.
  • Built a custom LMS landing page and reporting dashboard for the engagement.

Governance and stakeholder alignment

I turned an undefined engagement into a managed delivery plan with explicit scope, ownership, review responsibilities, and weekly internal and customer communication. This created the clarity needed to manage dependencies and deliver within six weeks.

Outcome

The team delivered 11 content items across two role-based paths in six weeks, earned 100% customer CSAT, and had the training recognized as a highlight of the onboarding retrospective.
