## Selected program case studies

### GitLab University launch

Led a one-quarter transformation of a limited certification site into a cohesive global learning ecosystem. I directed the program, redesigned and implemented the experience, curated more than 50 launch resources, organized technical content around customer workstreams, and coordinated stakeholders across Marketing, Engineering, Customer Success, and Developer Relations.

**Result:** A unified learning destination supporting 80K+ users and reaching learners in more than 180 countries.

### CI Fundamentals transformation

Owned the end-to-end redesign of an outdated, low-completion curriculum. I established a two-week sprint cadence, partnered with subject-matter experts, and released five modular courses combining video, hands-on labs, and realistic technical scenarios.

**Result:** Completion increased from 29% to 68%, and the modular learning-path format was adopted across the department.

### Cloud-migration onboarding

Recovered a high-visibility customer engagement that lacked defined curriculum, scope, or ownership. I created two role-based learning paths, coordinated internal and customer stakeholders, led two instructional designers, and managed weekly delivery updates.

**Result:** Delivered 11 resources in six weeks, earned 100% customer CSAT, and had the training recognized as a highlight of the onboarding engagement.

### GitLab Duo AI enablement

Designed a modular enablement model for a rapidly evolving AI product. The program emphasized durable customer outcomes over unstable feature details and introduced preconfigured hands-on labs for a more consistent learner experience.

**Result:** Created a scalable approach that made fast-changing AI learning content easier to release, update, and maintain.
