AI enablement · Change-ready design

GitLab Duo AI enablement program

I created a modular, outcome-led enablement model that could keep pace with a rapidly changing AI product while improving the consistency of hands-on practice.

Product area
GitLab Duo AI
Design principle
Customer outcomes over transient features
Delivery model
Modular, independently maintainable content
Innovation
Prepopulated hands-on lab projects

The challenge

Customers needed enablement quickly, but the GitLab Duo product was changing rapidly and many features were still experimental. A conventional feature-by-feature curriculum would become outdated quickly and create a heavy maintenance burden.

Program strategy

I designed the content as modular components that could be updated independently and centered the program on durable customer outcomes: what learners needed to accomplish, not simply which product controls existed at a particular moment.

Experience innovation

This was the first program in which our team used hands-on labs with a prepopulated project in the lab environment. The approach reduced setup variance, gave learners a consistent starting point, and focused practice on the intended technical workflow.

Change management and maintainability

I reduced the operational risk of rapid product change by separating the program into independently maintainable modules and anchoring learning to durable customer outcomes rather than transient feature details.

Strategic value

The resulting approach balanced speed, maintainability, and learning quality while establishing a reusable model for future AI enablement programs.
