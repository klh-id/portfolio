Platform launch · Global customer education

GitLab University launch

I led a one-quarter transformation of a low-traffic certification site into a branded learning ecosystem with more than 50 curated resources for a global technical audience.

Timeline
One quarter
Role
Program lead, UX designer, site implementer, content curator
Partners
Marketing, Engineering, Customer Success, Developer Relations
Reach
180+ countries

The opportunity

The existing Learn site received low traffic and offered very few instructional resources. Its catalog was centered on certification exams and preparation courses, which did not represent the broader ecosystem of technical education available to customers. The third-party design also felt disconnected from GitLab.

Program strategy

I positioned GitLab University as more than a rebrand. The launch would expand the content library, create a cohesive GitLab-owned experience, and help users find role-relevant resources faster. I reorganized the information architecture around technical workstreams including AI, CI/CD, project management, and security.

Leadership and execution

  • Led the project from definition through launch.
  • Designed the UI/UX and implemented the experience with custom CSS.
  • Curated and organized more than 50 learning resources.
  • Managed the work in GitLab across four cross-functional partner groups.
  • Provided weekly updates to the Senior Director of Customer Education and VP of Customer Success.

Governance and operating cadence

I managed the cross-functional work in GitLab, maintained a fixed quarterly launch window, and used weekly executive updates to create clarity around scope, progress, dependencies, and launch readiness.

Outcome

The program launched with more than 50 curated resources, presented a unified GitLab learning experience, and expanded global reach to learners in more than 180 countries.
