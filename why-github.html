Looking for a long-term fit

Why GitHub?

For my next move, the company matters just as much as the role. I’m looking for a place to stay and grow, and I’m specifically interested in GitHub.

What draws me here

My most recent experience has made me more intentional about company culture and values. I want a long-term fit: a company whose way of working feels natural to me, where I care about the product and enjoy the people I work with.

GitHub stands out for those reasons. Its values align with how I like to work, and I love remote work. I’m interested in the product and my time at GitLab gave me a strong understanding of the DevOps space and the challenges people face when learning these tools.

Everyone I’ve met with at GitHub has left a strong impression. Those conversations have made me more interested in joining the team. I’m being deliberate about this next step: finding the right company matters at least as much to me as finding the right role and GitHub is where I want to focus.
